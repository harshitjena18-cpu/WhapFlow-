## 2025-05-22 - ARIA Label Shadowing and Focus Contrast
**Learning:** `aria-label` on a button or link completely overrides its internal text content, including `sr-only` spans, making them invisible to screen readers. Additionally, focus indicators using brand colors at low opacity (e.g., 20%) often fail WCAG contrast requirements against light backgrounds.
**Action:** Use dynamic functional `aria-label` attributes (e.g., `aria-label="Notifications (1 unread)"`) for stateful icon buttons. Standardize focus rings at a minimum of 50% opacity for the brand green (#25D366) to ensure accessibility.
